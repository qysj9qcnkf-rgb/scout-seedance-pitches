[Story]
At night a woman at home is one click from buying a candle, wonders if it is safe for her cat, finds no answer and leaves; next morning its maker reads the low sales as nobody wanting it, until Scout's orb tells her not to guess. Live-action, warm, close framing, hard cuts, one overhead flip.
