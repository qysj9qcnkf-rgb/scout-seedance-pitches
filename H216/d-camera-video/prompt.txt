A warm live-action story ad in two homes for a small candle brand, 10 shots. Camera moves are the technique: the High flip is taken from a reference clip (@Video2, its move only); every other move is written in words.

Cast and places
The shopper (@Image1): dark-brown bob with bangs, cream cardigan. Her cat (@Image2), a grey tabby. Her night apartment (@Image3): candles burning on every surface; her laptop is open on her lap (not the closed laptop in the picture). Her laptop shows the Fig & Cedar product page (@Image4); she never clicks Add to cart (no cursor).
The owner (@Image5): auburn side braid, rust sweater. Her candle studio (@Image6). Her laptop shows units sold (@Image7), Fig & Cedar at 3 (-84%). The one Fig & Cedar jar (@Image8), lit, on her desk.
Voices: shopper @Audio1, owner @Audio2, Scout @Audio3 (timbre only, not their words). End screen @Video1.

Shots
1. 0-3s, opening, wide, slow arc toward her: the shopper on the sofa among the candles, laptop open, the cat curled beside her.
2. 3-5s, hard cut, over her shoulder: her fingers scroll the product page down, then back up, searching.
3. 5-8s, hard cut, close-up, the camera slowly circles her face, the cat soft behind. Inner voice, lips closed: {Are these safe for cats?}
4. 8-10s, hard cut, medium close-up, slow push in: she scrolls faster up and down, finds nothing about pets; jaw tightens, sharp exhale, rubs her brow. Silent.
5. 10-13s, hard cut, medium: she closes the site to an empty browser, leans back, looks at the cat. Inner voice, lips closed: {I guess we'll never know.}
6. 13-15s, High flip (@Video2): the camera rises over her until it looks straight down, the back of the sofa sweeps across the frame as the view flips, and it comes out level on the owner at her desk.
7. 15-21s, lands from the flip, medium close-up, slow push in: the owner reads her sales, Fig & Cedar at 3, the lit jar beside the laptop. She sighs, on camera: {I guess no one's interested in Fig and Cedar. No one wants this. Time to discontinue the product line.}
8. 21-23s, hard cut, close-up: she leans in and blows out the Fig & Cedar candle; the three flames go out, smoke rises.
9. 23-25s, hard cut, macro on the smoking wicks, slow push in, the studio dims. Scout, off screen: {Don't guess why your shoppers don't buy.}
10. 25-30s, hard cut, the end screen (@Video1), its picture and voice: {Scout interviews your shoppers for you. Get free early access.}

Sound: quiet night room tone and faint candle crackle at hers, quiet studio tone at the owner's, a soft warm music bed. No ka-ching, no cart sound, no chime. No subtitles. Hard cuts only, except the High flip.
