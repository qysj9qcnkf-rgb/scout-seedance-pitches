【Generation Goal】
Generate a realistic, warm short film in two homes. The core subjects are a shopper and the owner of the small candle brand Ember & Fig, and the principal event is a sale lost to one unanswered question: late at night the shopper almost buys a candle and quietly gives up because nothing tells her if it is safe for her cat; later, in her bright, sunlit candle studio, the owner reads the slow sales as nobody wanting that candle, until a glowing lime orb, Scout, stops her guess. Please note that this is not video editing.
