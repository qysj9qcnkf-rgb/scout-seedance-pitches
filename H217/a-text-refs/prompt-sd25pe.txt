【Generation Goal】
Generate a live-action story ad for Scout. The owner of the online bikini store Salt & Sun Swim hears a refund and nearly runs a discount, until Scout, a floating lime orb, freezes the moment and shows her the memory of a past call with a shopper who only waits for her next sale; she drops the discount and sells at full price. Please note that this is not video editing.
