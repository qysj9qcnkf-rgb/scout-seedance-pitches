【Generation Goal】
Generate a photoreal, full-colour story ad. A bikini-store owner gets a refund and is about to post a discount when Scout, a floating lime orb, freezes time and shows her a shopper who is just waiting for that discount.

【Reference Asset Roles】
Image 1 is a 12-panel line-art storyboard. Read it left to right, top to bottom, one panel per shot. Use it only for shot order, blocking and what each person holds; do not adopt its line-art style.
Image 2 is the refund notification on her phone: show it word for word.
Image 3 is the owner: use her face, hair and clothes.
Image 4 is her studio, the room for every owner shot.
Image 5 is the shopper and her bedroom.
Image 6 is the new-order notification on her phone: show it word for word.
Video 1 is the final shot: reproduce its picture. Scout's end line below replaces its sound. Scout uses the voice timbre of Video 1.
Audio 1 is the order sound.
Audio 2 is the owner's voice timbre only. Audio 3 is the shopper's voice timbre only.

【Subjects】
The owner (Image 3) appears only in her studio (Image 4), always with her own swimwear in her hands, her face, hair and clothes identical in every shot.
The shopper (Image 5) appears only in her bedroom, 13-21s.
Scout is one glowing lime orb with white sound bars, no face or body, first seen at 8s.

【Event Script】
Construct the story according to the storyboard sequence; the shots add what the panels don't show.

0-2s | Panel 1: Macro on her phone among folded bikinis. The notification from Image 2 drops in with Audio 1.

2-4s | Panel 2: Close-up. A bikini top in one hand, she frowns at the phone in the other.
The owner's line (annoyed but disappointed): {Oh, for... A refund.}

4-6s | Panel 3: Close-up to the lens, a stack of folded swimsuits in her hands with the phone on top.
The owner's line (flat, disappointed): {Sales are so low this week.}

6-8s | Panel 4: Over her shoulder at the discount form on her laptop, a folded suit in her hand. Room sound drops away; her lips stay closed.
The owner (V.O., inner voice): {Maybe it's time for a discount.}

8-11s | Panel 5: Bullet time: everything freezes. A tossed bikini hangs mid-air above her, and the camera arcs around her frozen figure. Scout appears for the first time, popping in by her shoulder with a soft <pop>.
Scout's line (calm, a little playful): {Not yet. There's something you don't know.}

11-13s | Panel 6: 11-12s, close-up, she turns to the orb, curious, a bikini top dangling from her hand. At 12s the camera rises and flips overhead with a <whoosh>, landing in the shopper's bedroom at 13s.

13-17s | Panel 7: The shopper packs her beach bag, the coral bikini on the bed. The orb hovers beside her.
Scout's line (curious, friendly): {You left the Sunset bikini in your cart three times. What stopped you?}

17-21s | Panel 8: Close-up in profile. She keeps packing a towel into the bag, without looking up.
The shopper's line (casual, matter-of-fact): {You run a discount every week. I'm just waiting for it.}

21-23s | Panel 9: Back in the studio, close-up. She holds a bikini top out by its straps, half a laugh.
The owner's line (dawning, wry): {I'm training them to wait.}

23-24s | Panel 10: Over her shoulder, a folded suit in one hand; one finger deletes the discount and the form breaks apart.
The owner's line (decided): {Noted.}

24-26s | Panel 11: Later that week, golden light. She seals a mailer at the table as the notification from Image 6 lands on the phone in front of her with Audio 1; she speaks on the sound.
The owner's line (relieved, smiling): {Glad I asked.}

26-30s | Panel 12: The orb becomes the "o" of the Scout wordmark, as in Video 1.
Scout's line (warm): {Scout interviews your shoppers for you. Get free early access.}

【Camera and Pace】
Hard cuts between shots, except the bullet-time arc at 8s and the overhead flip at 12s. A new visual every 2-3 seconds, fast, smooth, stabilised camera. Close framing: talking shots are close-ups; her hands and the swimwear fill the frame. Each line finishes inside its shot.

【Look and Sound】
Bright daylight, sand and sea-blue colours. (Light upbeat music, lower under speech.) All dialogue in English, strictly as written; whoever is not speaking keeps their mouth closed. No subtitles.
