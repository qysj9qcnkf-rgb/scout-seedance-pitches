【Generation Goal】
Generate a bright, slightly playful live-action ad. A woman who runs her own Shopify online store, all bikinis, is about to post a discount when Scout, a floating lime orb, freezes time and takes her back into a call Scout had with one of her shoppers, which changes her mind. Please note that this is not video editing.

Use Images 1 to 10 in order as keyframes. Each keyframe sets the composition, room, props and on-screen text of its own time range; move naturally from one keyframe to the next.
Use Image 1 as the first frame.

Audio 1 is the owner's voice: take only timbre, accent and pace; her words come only from this prompt.

Scout is the glowing lime-green orb in Images 4, 5 and 6, and the small lime badge beside the shopper's phone in Image 7.

【Subjects and Relationships】
The owner works alone in her sunlit studio, full of the bikinis she sells online: the same room in Images 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10. She wears the same loose white linen shirt in every studio shot.
The shopper exists only in the past, inside the orb (Image 6) and inside the memory (Image 7): her own bright bedroom, a striped tee, a straw beach bag she is packing, her phone at her ear.

The Sunset bikini is one orange-to-pink triangle bikini: the same one in the shopper's story and in the owner's hands.

【Event Script】
0-2s (reference: Image 1): Macro on her phone. <Audio 3 rings> as the "Refund issued" banner slides in.
2-4s (reference: Image 2): Hard cut. She reads the phone and deflates.
Owner's line (English; flat, dry sarcasm): {Oh, great. A refund.}
4-6s (reference: Image 2): Quick punch-in to a close-up; she looks into the lens.
Owner's line (English; wry, tired): {Sales are so low this week.}
6-8s (reference: Image 3): Hard cut over her shoulder. The room tone fades; her lips stay closed.
Owner (V.O., inner voice, English; tempted, close-mic): {Maybe it's time for a discount.}
8-10s (reference: Image 4): Everything freezes and the music stops: her hand stops on the laptop and the orange bikini top hangs in mid-air. <A soft pop> and Scout appears at her shoulder. Only the camera moves, gliding slowly around the frozen moment. Her lips stay closed.
Scout's line (English; calm, playful): {Not yet.}
10-12s (reference: Image 5): Reverse angle: she turns and looks at the orb, surprised and curious, lips closed.
Scout's line (English; warm, confident): {There's something you don't know.}
12-15s (reference: Image 6): The orb swells beside her into a round window onto the past. Inside it, the shopper sits on her bed packing a straw beach bag, her phone at her ear, on a call with Scout. The owner watches from her chair, chin on her hand, lips closed. The shopper listens, lips closed.
Scout's line (English; heard inside the orb from that call; curious, precise): {The Sunset bikini. Three times in your cart. What stopped you?}
15-18s (reference: Image 7): The camera pushes through the orb's glowing rim into that past moment: a lime glow around the edges of the frame, warm, soft light like a memory. The shopper smiles and shrugs, still packing.
Shopper's line (English; casual, easy): {You run a discount every week. I'm just waiting for it.}
18-21s (reference: Image 8): The lime glow snaps away: hard cut back to the studio, in the present. She holds the Sunset bikini top up by its strings in both hands, a rueful half-smile.
Owner's line (English; realising, quietly amused): {I'm training them to wait.}
21-23s (reference: Image 9): Close-up: she clicks Delete on the discount.
Owner's line (English; decisive, light): {Noted.}
23-25s (reference: Image 10): <Audio 3 rings> as the "New order" banner lands; she speaks right on the bell.
Owner's line (English; relieved, smiling): {Glad I asked.}
25-30s (reference: Video 1): Video 1 plays exactly as it is, with Scout's line from it: {Scout interviews your shoppers for you. Get free early access.} Its last frame holds until 30s.
